## What kind of bedrooms does this area have?

Two kinds. Aksarben Village is newer apartments and townhomes, so a visit is often one or two mattresses in a unit. The streets around Elmwood Park are older houses with upstairs bedrooms. UNO students, faculty and hospital staff live in both. We clean each mattress where it sits.

## How does a visit work?

A material inspection, low-moisture dry vapor steam on the surface, seams and tufts, HEPA vacuuming and UV-C light treatment, with a moisture check after the job and the built-in bed mite sensor on our UV-C vacuum.
