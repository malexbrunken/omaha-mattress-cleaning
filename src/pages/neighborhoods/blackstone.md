## What kind of bedrooms does Blackstone have?

Mostly apartments and condos, many in older buildings, plus older houses on the side streets. A Blackstone visit is often one or two mattresses up a flight of stairs, or a guest bed in a condo. We clean each mattress where it sits.

## How does a Blackstone visit work?

A material inspection, low-moisture dry vapor steam on the surface, seams and tufts, HEPA vacuuming and UV-C light treatment, with a moisture check after the job and the built-in bed mite sensor on our UV-C vacuum.

## Hosting guests near the medical center?

Blackstone is close to the hospital campuses, and many short-term rentals here host visiting families. Turnover timing and event weekends are on [Airbnb hosts](/airbnb-hosts).
