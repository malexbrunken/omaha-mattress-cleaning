## What kind of bedrooms does Benson have?

Mostly older one- and two-story houses on the streets around Maple, plus apartments above and near the business district. Older houses often mean small upstairs bedrooms and basement guest rooms. We clean each mattress where it sits, so tight corners and narrow stairs don't matter.

## How does a Benson visit work?

A material inspection, low-moisture dry vapor steam on the surface, seams and tufts, HEPA vacuuming and UV-C light treatment, with a moisture check after the job and the built-in bed mite sensor on our UV-C vacuum.
