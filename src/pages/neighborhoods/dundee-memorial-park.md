## What kind of bedrooms does Dundee have?

Mostly older houses. The district mixes single-family homes, duplexes and apartment buildings, many designed by leading Omaha architects of the early 1900s. Bedrooms are often upstairs, up a narrow staircase, and a third-floor or finished-attic guest room is common. That's no problem for us: we clean each mattress where it sits, so it never has to come down the stairs.

## How does a Dundee visit work?

A material inspection, low-moisture dry vapor steam on the surface, seams and tufts, HEPA vacuuming and UV-C light treatment, with a moisture check after the job and the built-in bed mite sensor on our UV-C vacuum.
