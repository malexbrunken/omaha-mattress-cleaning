## What kind of bedrooms does Millard have?

Every kind. The 1873 town grid around the old railroad still has its diagonal streets, renamed after annexation for Millard's pioneers, like Andresen, Cottner and Potwin. Around it sit the subdivisions built during the boom of the 1960s and the decades after. That means a Millard visit might be a kids' room in a split-level, a basement guest room or a main suite in a newer house. Multi-mattress visits are routine; rates are on [pricing](/pricing).

## How does a Millard visit work?

We clean each mattress where it sits: a material inspection, low-moisture dry vapor steam on the surface, seams and tufts, HEPA vacuuming and UV-C light treatment. Two checks come with every visit: a moisture check after the job and the built-in bed mite sensor on our UV-C vacuum.

## Where does an old Millard mattress go?

Millard is inside Omaha city limits, so the City of Omaha's programs apply. Our [Omaha disposal guide](/omaha-homes/getting-rid-of-a-mattress-in-omaha) covers Cleanup Saturdays, River City and Pheasant Point.
