## What does each trade actually do?

Carpet cleaners do good work on carpet, rugs and upholstery, and their equipment is built for floors. When a mattress is added to a carpet job, it is typically cleaned with that same hot-water extraction setup.

A mattress specialist starts from the bed. We identify the materials, then work low-moisture dry vapor steam into the seams, tufts, ridges and edges, where dust and shed skin flakes collect. HEPA vacuuming and UV-C light treatment follow on every visit, and we finish with a moisture check. Our UV-C vacuum also has a built-in bed mite sensor.

## When is a carpet cleaner the better hire?

When the job is floors. If you need wall-to-wall carpet, area rugs or a sofa cleaned, call a carpet or upholstery company. We don't do those, and we would rather say so than stretch our equipment past the bed.

## What should you ask before you book anyone?

- Is the mattress its own appointment, or an add-on to the floors?
- What equipment touches the mattress, and how much water goes in?
- Do you treat the seams, tufts and edges?
- What is included in the price, and is urine or pet odor extra?
- How do you confirm the mattress is dry to the touch before the bed is made?
