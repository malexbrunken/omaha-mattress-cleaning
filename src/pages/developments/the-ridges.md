## What kind of bedrooms do Ridges homes usually have?

Large primary suites plus several secondary bedrooms are common. That often means a multi-mattress visit in one stop. Each additional full, queen or king mattress in the same visit is $199; each additional kids bed (twin/full) is $149. See [pricing](/pricing).

## When do Ridges owners book?

Before a house goes on the market, after a pet accident, at annual maintenance, or when a guest suite sits closed most of the year. Hosts who list near Shadow Ridge should leave the bed unmade until it is dry to the touch after a visit; we run a moisture check after the job. Event timing for metro hosts is under [Airbnb hosts](/airbnb-hosts).

## How does travel work here?

We are a mobile crew. Give the street address when you book. The Ridges is west Omaha, well inside the area we already cover on [areas we serve](/areas).

## What about bed mites and method?

Bed mites (house dust mites) favor closed bedrooms. Method detail lives in the [Sleep Sanitation Knowledge Center](https://sleepsanitation.com/knowledge-center); this page is about where The Ridges sits and when to book.
