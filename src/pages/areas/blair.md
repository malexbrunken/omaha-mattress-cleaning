## Blair is in our service area

Blair is the Washington County seat, north of Omaha. We drive up and clean Blair mattresses Monday to Friday at the same published prices as Omaha. Book online or call or text (402) 512-5658.

## Blair housing by the numbers

The Census Bureau's 2020 to 2024 survey counts 3,285 housing units in Blair. The median year built is 1965, plus or minus six years, and about 25% of units were built before 1940. About 73% are single-family detached houses, and only about 2% were built in 2010 or later.

That makes Blair one of the older housing stocks on our list, much like [Council Bluffs](/areas/council-bluffs). Expect upstairs bedrooms and basement guest rooms; we clean every mattress where it sits.

## Where a Blair mattress can go

Blair doesn't run city trash collection. Residents contract with one of the city's licensed haulers, and the haulers take what they collect to the Douglas County landfill. Ask your hauler whether it takes a mattress as a bulk item and what it charges.

The free route is the city's Spring Cleanup at the Public Works yard, Third Avenue and Colfax Street. In 2026 it ran April 10 to 26, for Blair residents only, with proof of residency. The 2025 notice listed mattresses and box springs as accepted.

## What we don't know

- **Hauler mattress fees.** None of the three licensed haulers posts a bulk mattress price on the city's page.
- **The 2027 Spring Cleanup list.** The city publishes it each spring.
