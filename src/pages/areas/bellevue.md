## Bellevue's mattress rules, step by step

Bellevue is the one Sarpy County city on our list with a single citywide hauler. The city's contract with Papillion Sanitation covers single-family and duplex homes inside the city limits, and the welcome packet spells out what happens to items that don't fit in the blue landfill cart:

1. **Small extras go on a free bulk pickup.** Each household gets two a year, for items up to 60 pounds and 4 feet by 2 feet, scheduled ahead with Papillion Sanitation.
2. **A mattress is an oversized item.** The packet names couches and mattresses as items over those limits. Papillion Sanitation will schedule a large item curbside pickup year round, for a fee.
3. **Schedule it, or it stays.** Items set out without a scheduled pickup aren't collected.
4. **Or wait for a cleanup day.** The city holds two annual cleanup days where oversized items can be dropped off with no extra fee. The spring 2026 date was May 9, 7 a.m. to 3 p.m., at the Street Department yards at 29th and Hancock and at 8252 Cedar Island Road.

Papillion Sanitation's general residential page adds one more condition for every bulk pickup: mattresses and box springs must be wrapped in plastic.

## Other Bellevue drop-offs, for orientation

Bellevue's free tree dump, just north of 8902 Cedar Island Road, opens the last Saturday of each month from 8 a.m. to noon, for tree debris only. Glass recycling goes to the District 3 Fire Station lot at 9400 S 36th Street. Neither takes a mattress, but both help you place the Cedar Island Road cleanup site.

## Bellevue housing by the numbers

Bellevue has 26,560 housing units in the Census Bureau's 2020 to 2024 survey, with a median year built of 1982, plus or minus two years. About 28% were built before 1970, the second-highest share on our area list after Ralston, and about 25% in 2000 or later. Roughly 68% are single-family detached houses.

A 1960s ranch and a 2015 build get the same cleaning; the mattress, not the house, sets the method. What older Bellevue housing can change is the bedroom itself. If yours is in a lower level, read our [Omaha basement humidity guide](/omaha-homes/humid-summers-and-basement-bedrooms) before scheduling summer work. The survey doesn't count basements, so we can't say how common they are in Bellevue.

## Pests, odor and the county

Bellevue residents bring indoor nuisance complaints to the Sarpy/Cass Health Department, which handles them within Sarpy and Cass counties. Suspected bed bugs go to a licensed pest professional first; our cleaning follows treatment. For a pet accident on a Bellevue mattress, Sleep Sanitation's [urine, pet accident and odor guide](https://sleepsanitation.com/knowledge-center/mattress-accidents/mattress-urine-pet-accidents-and-odor-guide) explains when cleaning helps.
