## Same crew, same price, other side of the river

Council Bluffs is in Iowa, but for us it's part of the same metro. We cross the Missouri on I-480 or I-80 and clean Council Bluffs mattresses Monday to Friday at the same published prices as Omaha. Book online or call or text (402) 512-5658.

## Council Bluffs housing by the numbers

The Census Bureau's 2020 to 2024 survey counts 27,616 housing units in Council Bluffs. The median year built is 1965, plus or minus two years, and about 26% of units were built before 1940, compared with Omaha's median of 1973. About 69% are single-family detached houses, and only about 6% were built in 2010 or later.

Older houses mean upstairs bedrooms, narrow staircases and basement guest rooms. We clean every mattress where it sits, so none of that matters. Low river-bottom humidity in summer is a factor too; see [Missouri River bottom humidity](/omaha-homes/missouri-river-bottom-humidity).

## If the mattress is leaving instead

Council Bluffs handles mattresses differently from Omaha, with a twice-a-year bulky pickup and a pay-by-weight drop-off. We compare the two cities side by side in [Council Bluffs vs Omaha mattress disposal](/omaha-homes/council-bluffs-vs-omaha-mattress-disposal).

## Bed bugs in Pottawattamie County

The Iowa Department of Health and Human Services publishes a bed bug FAQ, and Pottawattamie County Public Health is at 515 5th Avenue, (712) 242-1155. Treatment is a job for a licensed pest professional; our cleaning comes after.
