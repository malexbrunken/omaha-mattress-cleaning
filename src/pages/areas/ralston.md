## Ralston's trash setup

The City of Ralston's trash page fits in a few lines. The city contracts with Papillion Sanitation for trash and recycling, and trash service is included in your water bill. Grass and leaves go out during two windows, the last two weeks of May and again in November. When a holiday closes the landfill, pickup slides a day.

A mattress doesn't fit any of that. Papillion Sanitation's residential page says it takes furniture and mattresses that don't fit in a cart on a separate, scheduled bulk route, for an extra fee, and that mattresses and box springs must be wrapped in plastic first. Call Papillion Sanitation before the mattress goes to the curb; Ralston's page sends hauler questions there.

Because Ralston is a separate city, Omaha's free Cleanup Saturdays are not an option. Wasteline opens them to City of Omaha households, and a Ralston address isn't one.

## Ralston housing by the numbers

Ralston's housing is the oldest on our area list. The Census Bureau's 2020 to 2024 survey puts the median year built for its 2,987 housing units at 1972, plus or minus three years, nearly identical to Omaha's 1973. About 45% were built before 1970, about 22% before 1960, and only about 18% in 2000 or later.

An older Ralston house doesn't mean an older mattress, and it doesn't change how we clean one. Where it can matter is getting a mattress out for disposal, so measure the stairway before you buy a replacement. The survey doesn't record basements, so we can't tell you how many Ralston bedrooms are below grade. If yours is, our [Omaha basement humidity guide](/omaha-homes/humid-summers-and-basement-bedrooms) covers summer conditions down there.

## Bed bugs and the Douglas County Health Department

Ralston is in Douglas County, so the Douglas County Health Department is the county agency. Its pest page says bed bugs have not been shown to be a vector of disease and that the department's role with them is education. In a Ralston home that leaves the job to a licensed pest professional, with cleaning after treatment. Sleep Sanitation's guide to [dust mites, bed bugs and bed mites](https://sleepsanitation.com/knowledge-center/mattress-dust-mites-allergens/dust-mites-vs-bed-bugs-vs-bed-mites) sorts out which is which. This page is general information, not medical advice.

## Finding Ralston, for orientation

Ralston City Hall is at 5500 S 77th Street, with the Baright Public Library next door at 5555 S 77th Street. Ralston Public Works is at 8220 Serum Ave., open weekdays.

## What a Ralston job includes

Our Ralston rate is the same as everywhere: $249 for the first mattress, or $199 under the Fall 2026 first-mattress offer. The technician wears gloves and shoe booties, and every piece of equipment is disinfected between jobs. We run three checks and only three: a moisture test after the job, a bedroom CO2 test, and the built-in dust mite sensor on our UV-C vacuum. The inspection form for your Ralston job records material, special care notes and urine or odor observations. Check your care label and warranty terms first.
