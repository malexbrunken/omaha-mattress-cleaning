## Berkshire's weekend, as it ran in 2026

Berkshire Hathaway's 2026 shareholder weekend filled three days: a shopping day at CHI Health Center on Friday, May 1 from noon to 5pm, the meeting on Saturday, May 2 with doors opening at 7am and the formal annual meeting at 2pm, and the Invest in Yourself 5K at Lewis & Clark Landing on Sunday, May 3. A shareholder who comes for Friday's shopping day and stays for Sunday's run books three nights, so for Berkshire 2026 the useful weekday windows were the week of April 27 and Monday, May 4 onward.
