## What Offutt's lodging office lists

- **Air Force Inns** runs Offutt's temporary lodging from 906 SAC Boulevard; Military OneSource lists reservations at 402-281-1292 and a reception desk open 8am to 8pm, seven days a week.
- **13 three-bedroom units** at Rising View, in the Capehart military housing area, each furnished with Wi-Fi and cable; 4 are pet-friendly, and Military OneSource says pet options at Offutt are limited.
- **May through October** are the peak transition months, when PCS moves in or out can outnumber the units.
- **Confirmation:** Military OneSource says a guest must call Air Force Inns 24 hours before arrival to confirm, even with a reservation.

## Who's on the base

Military OneSource's Offutt overview, citing the 2024 Economic Impact Statement, puts the base's total population at 44,914, including 6,980 military members and 8,096 dependents. Offutt hosts the 55th Wing, US Strategic Command, the 557th Weather Wing and the 95th Wing. We don't know how many Offutt visitors book private rentals, so treat these as base figures, not a forecast for your listing.

## From Eppley to the gate

Military OneSource's directions send drivers south on US 75 toward Bellevue to the Kenney Gate, or on to the Capehart Road exit for the SAC Gate, where the Visitor's Center is. Offutt sits in Sarpy County, where DOR lists a 4% county lodging tax since April 1, 2009 and Bellevue's city sales tax at 1.5%; our [lodging tax guide](/airbnb-hosts/omaha-lodging-taxes-and-direct-bookings) covers the 30-day line and direct bookings.

## Scheduling around a long Offutt stay

Book the cleaning for a weekday after the family checks out and before your next guest. Across PCS season, Eppley's average dew point runs from 50°F in May to 66°F in July and 55°F in September, so keep the bedroom closed and the air conditioning on afterwards.
