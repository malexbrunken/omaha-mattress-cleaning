## What Offutt's lodging office lists

- **Air Force Inns** runs Offutt's temporary lodging from 906 SAC Boulevard.
- **13 three-bedroom units**, 4 of them pet-friendly, according to Military OneSource on October 3, 2026.
- **May through October** are the peak transition months, when permanent change of station moves can outnumber the units.
- **Reservations** should be made well ahead, and Military OneSource says a guest must call 24 hours before arrival to confirm, even with a reservation.

## A PCS stay is a different kind of booking

A family between houses in PCS season often arrives with luggage for weeks, sometimes a pet, and a move-in date that slips. For the bed, that means pet odor and accident questions more often than a CWS weekend would bring, and a stay long enough that the guest's own routine shapes the room. We note what we find on the inspection form at the next weekday visit.

## Sarpy County, Bellevue and the 30-day line

Offutt sits in Bellevue, in Sarpy County, which has charged a 4% county lodging tax since April 1, 2009; DOR's July 1, 2026 table lists Bellevue's city sales tax at 1.5%. GIL 1-19-1 treats a short-term rental as a stay of less than 30 days, and DOR's guide 5-141 says a credit for a longer stay depends on an agreement from the first day of occupancy. If a PCS family books 45 nights, settle the tax question with DOR before the first night.
