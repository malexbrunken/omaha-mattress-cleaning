## Side by side

| | Council Bluffs | Omaha |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Curbside mattress pickup | Yes, by appointment, two per year | No |
| Wrapping rule | Bagged or completely shrink wrapped | None published for drop-off |
| Size limit | One pile up to 8 ft long, 4 ft wide, 4 ft high | Not applicable |
| Drop-off | Recycling Center, 4441 Gifford Rd | River City, 6404 S 60th St; Pheasant Point, Hwy 36 and 216th St |
| Drop-off cost | $75 per ton prorated, $10 minimum | City program: $15 a year for 4 vouchers (residents only) |
| Free event | None found on the city pages we checked | Cleanup Saturdays, spring and fall |

## Council Bluffs checklist

- **Call Waste Connections at (712) 890-5549** to book one of your two yearly bulky pickups. The city allows one scheduled pickup per collection day.
- **Wrap the mattress.** A mattress bag or full shrink wrap; the box spring too.
- **Stay inside the pile limit.** 8 feet by 4 feet by 4 feet, which the city compares to a full-size couch.
- **Place it neatly at the curb** on the scheduled day.
- **Used both pickups?** Haul it to the Recycling Center on Gifford Road and pay by weight, with a $10 minimum.

## Omaha checklist

The short version is below; the details, including River City's residency proof and hours, are on our [Omaha disposal guide](/omaha-homes/getting-rid-of-a-mattress-in-omaha).

- **October 2026:** four free Cleanup Saturdays, one area of the city each week.
- **Any other time:** River City under the city program if you live inside Omaha city limits, or Pheasant Point.

## Before you wrap it

A mattress sealed in plastic for disposal is the end of the road. If what bothers you is a stain, an odor or a bed you bought used, that is a cleaning question, and Sleep Sanitation's [guide to sanitizing a used mattress](https://sleepsanitation.com/knowledge-center/mattress-owners-decision-center/can-a-used-mattress-be-sanitized) covers it. Our Omaha service covers Council Bluffs too.

## What we don't know

- **Whether Omaha's Cleanup sites turn away out-of-city residents at the gate.** The city says the event is for City of Omaha households; we haven't seen how it is checked.
- **Council Bluffs pickup lead time.** The city doesn't publish how far ahead to book.
