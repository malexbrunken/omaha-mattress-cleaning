## Why does new-construction dust hit mattresses?

Finish dust floats, then settles into soft surfaces. A mattress that sat through sanding and carpet install holds that load until someone sleeps on it. We clean after the dust phase ends, not during it.

## Which west Omaha corridors book this most?

Elkhorn acreage plats, 168th Street builder communities and northwest golf neighborhoods. See [The Sanctuary](/developments/the-sanctuary), [Tiburon](/developments/tiburon) and the [developments hub](/developments).

## What should I do before you arrive?

Strip bedding, clear nightstands, and finish floor vacuuming. Leave the bed unmade after we leave until it is dry to the touch; we run a moisture check after the job.

## Where is the science?

Method and bed mites (house dust mites) live in the [Sleep Sanitation Knowledge Center](https://sleepsanitation.com/knowledge-center). This page is about timing after a west Omaha build.

## What we don't know

- **Your builder's exact dust-control plan.** Wait until rooms are finished.
- **Whether foam needs underside work.** We check the care label on site.
