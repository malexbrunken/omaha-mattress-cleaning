## Why separate PCS homeowners from Offutt hosts?

Hosts book around guest calendars. PCS homeowners book around a move date for beds the family actually slept on. Different timing, same $249 first-mattress rate.

## When in the PCS timeline should I book?

After boxes leave the bedrooms at move-out, or after furniture is placed at move-in — before the first overnight if you can. Leave the bed unmade until it is dry to the touch after we finish.

## What about pets and kids beds?

Additional mattresses in the same visit are labeled on [pricing](/pricing). Tell us about pet urine when you book so we bring the right chemistry.
