## Omaha's dry season, month by month

| Month | Avg dew point | Avg temperature | At 70°F indoors |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| November | 29°F | 39°F | about 22% |
| December | 20°F | 28°F | about 15% |
| January | 16°F | 24°F | about 13% |
| February | 19°F | 28°F | about 15% |
| March | 28°F | 40°F | about 21% |

Dew points and temperatures are our averages of NOAA's 1991 to 2020 hourly normals for Eppley Airfield, rounded. The last column is our calculation of what that outdoor air reads once the furnace warms it to 70°F. It is an estimate, not a measurement of any house. Showers, cooking and a humidifier all add moisture, so a hygrometer is the only way to know what your bedroom reads.

## Setting the humidifier

- **Aim for 30 to 45 percent.** That is Nebraska Extension's range to control house dust mites, and it stays under the EPA's 50 percent ceiling.
- **Watch the windows.** The EPA says to move the humidifier, lower its setting or run it less if water condenses on windows, walls or pictures. On a January night at Eppley's average of 24°F, cold glass shows it first.
- **Keep it off the bed.** The EPA says not to let absorbent materials around the humidifier get damp. Set it across the room, not on the nightstand.
- **Clean the tank.** The EPA's tips for portable units are to empty and refill the tank daily, clean it every third day and use low-mineral water such as distilled.
