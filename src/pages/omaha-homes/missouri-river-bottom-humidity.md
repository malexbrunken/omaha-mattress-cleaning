## How is river-bottom humidity different?

Floodplain air and soils stay wetter after storms. A closed guest room near the river can feel musty even when inland west Omaha rooms feel fine the same week. Bed mites (house dust mites) favor those closed, humid beds.

## When should bottoms owners book?

After a humid stretch, before guests, or when a spare room sat closed through July–August. Pair with your dehumidifier — we do not replace building moisture control.

## What about elkhorn river homes?

Elkhorn River acreage (for example [The Sanctuary](/developments/the-sanctuary)) has its own valley humidity. Use that development page for travel notes; use this page when the address is Missouri floodplain / bottoms.

## What we don't know

- **Your FEMA flood zone or sump status.** Tell us about recent water in the bedroom before we book.
