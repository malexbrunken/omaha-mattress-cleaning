| Omaha Mattress Cleaning (us) | Cleans and sanitizes the mattress after treatment is complete | Kill or eliminate a bed bug infestation |

## What should you do if you think you have bed bugs?

- **Look before you act.** Nebraska Extension lists the signs: live bugs, dead bugs, shed skins and black fecal marks, starting in seams, folds and zippers.
- **Get it identified.** Extension notes that bat bugs look nearly identical and are managed differently.
- **Call a licensed pest professional** to inspect and treat.
- **Book cleaning last.** We schedule after treatment is complete and the pest professional has finished follow-up.

## What about bites?

Bites affect people differently, and Nebraska DHHS says bed bugs are not known to spread disease but that an allergic reaction to several bites may need medical attention. This page is general information, not medical advice; talk to your doctor about bites.
