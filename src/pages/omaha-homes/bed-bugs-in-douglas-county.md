## Who does what in Omaha

| Who | What they do | What they don't do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Douglas County Health Department, (402) 444-7485 | Bed bug education; takes Douglas County complaints about other pests and nuisances | Treat bed bugs |
| Your landlord (if you rent) | Receives your notice and keeps the unit fit and habitable under Neb. Rev. Stat. 76-1419 and your lease | Give legal advice |
| Licensed pest professional | Inspects, identifies and treats, with the follow-up visits Nebraska Extension describes | Clean your mattress |
| Omaha Mattress Cleaning (us) | Cleans and sanitizes the mattress after treatment is complete | Kill or eliminate a bed bug infestation |

## If you think you have bed bugs

- **Look before you act.** Nebraska Extension lists the signs: live bugs, dead bugs, shed skins and black fecal marks, starting in seams, folds and zippers.
- **Get it identified.** Extension notes that bat bugs look nearly identical and are managed differently.
- **Tell your landlord in writing.** State law ties the landlord's duty to written or actual notice, and a dated message is a record.
- **Call a licensed pest professional** if you own the home, or once your landlord has arranged one.
- **Book cleaning last.** We schedule after treatment is complete and the pest professional has finished follow-up.

## Health questions

Bites affect people differently, and Nebraska DHHS says bed bugs are not known to spread disease but that an allergic reaction to several bites may need medical attention. This page is general information, not medical or legal advice; talk to your doctor about symptoms and to a lawyer or tenant resource about your lease.
