## Pick your route

| Route | Who can use it | Cost | When |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cleanup Saturday | City of Omaha households, residential items only | Free | Four October Saturdays in 2026; spring dates in April and May |
| River City city program | Residents inside Omaha city limits | $15 a year for 4 vouchers, plus extra fees for large or heavy loads | Year round |
| Pheasant Point Landfill | Anyone | Flat rates or special handling fees may apply; call first | Call for hours |

## Cleanup Saturday checklist

- **Find your Saturday.** Each date covers one part of the city: Southwest on October 3, Southeast on October 10, Northeast on October 17 and Northwest on October 24. The Wasteline page links a map and a site list.
- **Go between 9 AM and 2 PM.** That is the window the city publishes for every site.
- **Bring help.** Residents unload their own vehicles, and the city says no assistance is provided.
- **Keep it residential.** Commercial waste is prohibited.
- **Missed October?** The spring event runs on Saturdays in April and May. In 2026 the pattern started with Central on April 18 and ended with Southwest on May 16.

## River City checklist

- **Confirm your address first.** The program is for residents inside Omaha city limits; the Wasteline page has an address lookup.
- **Bring two proofs of residency** from different categories, such as a current state ID and a recent OPPD or MUD bill. Names and street addresses must match.
- **Ask for the "city program" before you dump.** The city says refunds are not given after you have already unloaded.
- **Tarp the load.** State and local law require a covered load.
- **Wear a vest and hard hat** if you step out to unload, or buy them there ($12 hard hat, $6 vest, as listed by the city).
- **Pay without cash.** River City is a cashless business.
- **Check hours by season, or call 402-889-8721** (the number on the City's Wasteline page). Easter to Thanksgiving: Monday to Friday 6 AM to 4 PM, Saturday 6 AM to 1 PM. Thanksgiving to Easter: Saturday hours shorten to 7 AM to 11:30 AM.

## When keeping it makes more sense

If the mattress is structurally sound and the problem is stains, odor or a used-bed history, cleaning is the other route. Sleep Sanitation's [clean, replace or hire guide](https://sleepsanitation.com/knowledge-center/mattress-owners-decision-center/mattress-owners-decision-guide-clean-replace-or-hire) walks through that decision; it isn't specific to Omaha, so we don't repeat it here. A sagging or broken bed is a disposal job, and in October the free Saturday is the cheapest exit.

If you suspect bed bugs, read [who does what about bed bugs in Douglas County](/omaha-homes/bed-bugs-in-douglas-county) before you carry the mattress anywhere.
