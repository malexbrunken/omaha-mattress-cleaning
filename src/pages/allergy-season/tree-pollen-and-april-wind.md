## Spring at Eppley Airfield

| Month | Average wind | Most common direction | Average dew point |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| March | 11.3 mph | North | 28°F |
| April | 11.8 mph | North | 38°F |
| May | 10.5 mph | Southeast | 50°F |

Our averages of NOAA's 1991 to 2020 hourly normals for Eppley Airfield. For comparison, August is the calmest month at 8.2 mph. NOAA's median date for the last 32°F night of spring at Eppley is April 18, so the trees are already releasing pollen while frost is still possible.

## Why April is the month to watch

In April, Poole's 2014 timeline puts Nebraska's trees in full swing by early to mid-April, and Eppley's normals make April the windiest month of the year. Rorie added in 2022 that wind changes where pollen comes from, not just how much there is: he noted that cedar pollen traced to Texas has been collected as far north as Ontario. So an April count in Omaha can include grains from well beyond the city.

## Windows, clothes and the bed

UNMC's spring advice is practical rather than medical:

- **Keep the bedroom windows closed.** Poole called tree pollen hardy and longer lasting once it gets inside, and said closed windows keep it out of the room where you sleep.
- **Change clothes after time outdoors**, as Poole advised in 2014, and wash your hands and face or shower before you lie down on the bed.
- **Watch Eppley's wind as well as UNMC's count.** A low count on a calm day and the same count on an 11 mph April afternoon aren't the same day outdoors.
