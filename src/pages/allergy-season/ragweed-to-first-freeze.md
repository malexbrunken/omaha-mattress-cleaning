## Late summer and fall at Eppley Airfield

| Month | Average dew point | Average wind | Freeze normals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| August | 64°F | 8.2 mph, the calmest month | None |
| September | 55°F | 9.2 mph | None |
| October | 41°F | 9.8 mph | Median first 32°F night October 21; first 28°F night October 30 |

Our averages of NOAA's 1991 to 2020 normals for Eppley Airfield. NOAA's median growing season there, between the last and first 32°F nights, is 185 days.

## What the weed line looked like in late September 2026

The Douglas County count fell across the week: 54 (high) on Monday, September 28, with hemp, nettle and ragweed named; 26 on Tuesday; 18 on Wednesday; 1 on Thursday, October 1; and back to 12 (moderate) on Friday, October 2, with pigweed and ragweed. That rebound is why a single quiet day isn't the end of the season, and why we'd watch the count rather than Eppley's freeze date.

## Three things to do before the freeze

- **Close the windows on days DCHD's bulletin says high.** UNMC's Andrew Rorie suggested closed house windows when counts are high.
- **Shower and change after yard work**, following UNMC's pollen season advice to change clothes and rinse off after time outdoors, so less of the day's weeds reaches the pillow.
- **Don't trust a calm month.** September averages 9.2 mph at Eppley, one of the calmer months, yet the weed count still reached 54 on September 28, 2026.

## Booking around the weed count

A fall mattress cleaning doesn't change DCHD's weed count, and we don't claim it helps allergies; if you want the appointment after the weed line has quieted down, book a weekday after Eppley's October 21 median first freeze and ask us to note it on the inspection form if someone in the house is pollen sensitive.
