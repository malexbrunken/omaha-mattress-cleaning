## Early summer at Eppley Airfield

| Month | Average dew point | Average temperature | Average wind |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| May | 50°F | 63°F | 10.5 mph |
| June | 61°F | 73°F | 9.7 mph |
| July | 66°F | 77°F | 8.5 mph |

Our averages of NOAA's 1991 to 2020 hourly normals. From June on, the most common wind direction at Eppley is south or southeast, and the air carries far more moisture than it did in April.

## The dog is part of the count

Rorie's 2022 advice was aimed at pet owners with pollen allergies: wipe the dog down with a wet towel whenever it comes back in, because tree and grass pollen ride in on its coat. A dog that sleeps on the comforter brings that coat to the mattress every night, which a rooftop count at Durham Research Center II can't see.

What helps on the bed side, in UNMC's 2022 terms, is ordinary: closed windows when its Tuesday or Thursday count runs high, a change of clothes after yard work, and a shower before bed.

## Where a cleaning fits

Pet odor and ordinary urine accidents on a dog's side of the bed are included in the first-mattress price on our [allergy season hub](/allergy-season#visit), and each goes on the inspection form before we HEPA vacuum and steam.

June's 61°F average dew point is a reason to run the air conditioning or a dehumidifier afterwards rather than opening windows. The EPA's mold guidance puts indoor relative humidity below 60 percent, ideally 30 to 50 percent.
