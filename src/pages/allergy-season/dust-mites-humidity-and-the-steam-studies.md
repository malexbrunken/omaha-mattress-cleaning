## Pollen is outdoors and seasonal; mites are indoors and year-round

| | Pollen | Bed mites |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where it's counted | DCHD's outdoor sampler, and rooftop samplers at UNMC and NAB Station 33 | Not in DCHD's, UNMC's or Station 33's counts |
| Season | Trees from March (UNMC); weeds still counted on October 2, 2026 (DCHD) | All 12 months, indoors |
| What moves it | Wind: April averages 11.8 mph at Eppley | Humidity in the room |

## Eppley's moisture year, outdoors and at 70°F

NOAA's 1991 to 2020 hourly normals put Eppley's average dew point at 16°F in January, 61°F in June, 66°F in July and 41°F in October. In Eppley's normal July, no hour has a dew point under 60°F; in June, 249 of the 720 hours do.

Using the standard Magnus approximation, we estimated what that outdoor air would read at a 70°F room temperature with no moisture added or removed: about 87 percent for July's 66°F dew point, 73 percent for June's 61°F and 13 percent for January's 16°F. That's why the EPA's 60 percent line is easiest to cross in an Omaha summer, and why an Eppley January runs dry indoors. These are estimates from Eppley's outdoor normals, so a hygrometer by the bed tells you more about your room.

## What the two steam studies measured, and what they didn't

- **Glass and Needham (2004):** a steam cleaner at 96°C, applied to carpet and mattress samples, left no surviving Dermatophagoides farinae mites in those samples; the abstract doesn't report how many samples were tested.
- **Colloff and colleagues (1995):** steam-treated carpet squares seeded with mites had no live mites over the following 4 months, which is carpet, not a mattress.

Neither study measured allergy symptoms, an Omaha bedroom or our equipment, so we cite them for the heat effect only; Sleep Sanitation's [steam and bed mite review](https://sleepsanitation.com/knowledge-center/mattress-dust-mites-allergens/does-steam-kill-dust-mites-in-mattresses) walks through both. Killing mites also isn't removing them: bodies and droppings can remain in a mattress, and we HEPA vacuum as well as steam, but we don't measure what the vacuum removes.
