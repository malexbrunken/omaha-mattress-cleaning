## Omaha's pollen year, start to finish

| Season | What's in the air | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| March to May | Tree pollen | UNMC |
| May to July | Grass pollen | UNMC |
| Mid-August on | Ragweed and other weeds | UNMC; DCHD weed counts |
| Late October | Eppley's median first 32°F night, October 21 | NOAA normals |
| Late October | Eppley's median first 28°F night, October 30 | NOAA normals |

## What the first freeze changes

A hard freeze ends the growing season for the weeds that drive fall counts. NOAA's normals give Eppley Airfield a median growing season of 185 days between the last and first 32°F nights. After that, the daily weed line in the NWS Omaha/Valley bulletin is the one to watch: when it stays low, the outdoor season is behind you.
