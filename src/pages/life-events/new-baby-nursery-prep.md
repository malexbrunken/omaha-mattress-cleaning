## Which beds should you clean before the baby comes?

The ones people sleep on: your own mattress, where night feeds and daytime naps for tired parents happen, and any older sibling's twin or full. If grandparents are coming to Omaha to help, the guest bed belongs on the list too; see [resetting the guest-room mattress](/life-events/holiday-guest-room-reset).

The crib is the exception. We leave it to the crib mattress manufacturer's label and to the safe-sleep guidance below.

## What does Nebraska's safe-sleep guidance say about the crib?

Nebraska DHHS teaches the ABCs: Alone, on the Back, in a Crib. Its Safe Sleep and SIDS page adds a firm, flat surface, room-sharing on the baby's own surface, and no soft objects, pillows, blankets or loose bedding in the sleep space. That is why we don't sell toppers, pads or anything else for a crib, and why a clean adult bed is never a reason to bed-share. The national version is NICHD's Safe to Sleep campaign.

## How does Omaha's weather change the timing?

Babies due in late spring or summer arrive in Omaha's most humid months. NOAA's normals put the average July dew point at Eppley near 66°F. That doesn't rule out a visit; it means keeping the air conditioning or a dehumidifier running, keeping the bed unmade until it is dry to the touch, and trusting the moisture check we run after service rather than an open window.

Winter babies have the opposite calendar problem: holiday weeks. Book before Thanksgiving week if the due date is in December.

## Is it time to replace a mattress instead?

If your mattress sags, smells musty after a basement summer, or has been through more than a cleaning can fix, we will say so during the inspection. Omaha's curbside carts don't take mattresses; the [Omaha mattress disposal guide](/omaha-homes/getting-rid-of-a-mattress-in-omaha) lists the drop-off options and Cleanup Saturdays.

## What happens during the visit?

A material inspection, low-moisture dry vapor steam on the surface, seams, tufts and edges, HEPA vacuuming and UV-C light treatment, with organic methods by default. Every visit includes a moisture check after service and the built-in bed mite sensor on our UV-C vacuum. The optional 72-hour bedroom CO₂ test is a separate service, priced by quote, and it isn't a medical test.
