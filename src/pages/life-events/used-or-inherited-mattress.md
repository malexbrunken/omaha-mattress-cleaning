## What should you do before the mattress comes inside?

Stand it up in the garage or driveway in daylight and look. The EPA says to check secondhand furniture, beds and couches for signs of bed bugs before bringing them home, and its signs list is short: live bugs, dark spots, rusty or reddish stains, and tiny pale eggs or shed skins, usually near the piping, seams and tags. A flashlight helps. If you find signs of bed bugs, stop there; cleaning isn't pest control, and our [Douglas County bed bug guide](/omaha-homes/bed-bugs-in-douglas-county) covers who handles what locally.

## Why does an Omaha hand-me-down need a closer look?

Because of where it has been. Omaha's median home was built in 1974, and 30% of the city's homes date from before 1960, per the Census Bureau's 2024 American Community Survey. Many inherited mattresses come out of those houses, often from a basement bedroom that saw years of Omaha summers. Omaha's July dew point averages about 66°F at Eppley, and a cool basement can hold that moisture; our [summer humidity and basement bedrooms](/omaha-homes/humid-summers-and-basement-bedrooms) guide explains why. So we inspect for moisture and odor before we treat.

## What happens if you decide not to keep it?

Omaha narrows the options. Habitat for Humanity ReStore Omaha's donation guidance says it does not take mattresses, box springs, futon mattresses or sleeper sofas. The city's curbside carts don't take mattresses either. The Omaha Recycling Guide points to River City Recycling, Pheasant Point or a Cleanup Saturday instead; fees and dates are in our [mattress disposal guide](/omaha-homes/getting-rid-of-a-mattress-in-omaha).

## What does a cleaning do for a mattress with an unknown history?

It resets what a cleaning can reset. We start with a material inspection and an inspection form, then low-moisture dry vapor steam on the surface, seams, tufts and edges, HEPA vacuuming, UV-C light treatment and enzyme treatment for urine or organic odor. Underside treatment is worth adding on a used mattress. Every visit includes a moisture check after service and the built-in bed mite sensor on our UV-C vacuum. The method itself is explained in the [Sleep Sanitation Knowledge Center](https://sleepsanitation.com/knowledge-center).
