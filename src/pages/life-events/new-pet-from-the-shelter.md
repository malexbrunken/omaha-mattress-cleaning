## Where should a new pet sleep in the first week?

Pick the spot before you drive home from 90th and Fort. Shelter pets are learning a new house, a new routine and new people at once, and accidents in the first weeks are common. A crate or dog bed in the bedroom, or a closed bedroom door, is easier than retraining later. If the pet will share the bed, put a waterproof mattress protector on first.

## What does Omaha require in the first 30 days?

A license. The Nebraska Humane Society runs pet licensing for the City of Omaha, including Elkhorn. Dogs, cats and mini pigs need a license at 4 months of age, and you have 30 days after getting a new pet. NHS needs proof of a current rabies vaccination, and the altered-pet rate is lower than the intact rate. Omaha allows 3 dogs and 5 cats per household without a pet avocation permit.

## What if someone in the house has allergies?

Go by the evidence, not the breed. The AAAAI says there are no truly hypoallergenic breeds of dog or cat, and that keeping pets out of the bedroom can reduce allergy symptoms because you spend about eight hours a day there. It also notes dander lingers on furniture. A mattress cleaning removes dust, hair and dander from the mattress surface, seams and tufts with HEPA vacuuming, but it isn't an allergy treatment; an allergist is.
