## When should you book the guest bed before the holidays?

Work back from the first arrival. If Thanksgiving guests land on Wednesday, November 25, 2026, the bed needs to be done before that week starts. The Holiday Lights Festival lighting ceremony downtown on Saturday, November 28 gives visiting family a reason to stay through the weekend, and the New Year's Eve fireworks on Thursday, December 31 can bring a second round of guests. A weekday visit in the first half of November covers both.

If you are hosting for Christmas only, the week of December 14 leaves time to make the bed and air the room. Christmas Day is a Friday in 2026, so the last full weekday week before it is December 14 to 18.

## What is different about a guest bed nobody slept on?

Less wear, not less dust. A spare room still gets household dust, and the last guest left their shed skin flakes behind. We treat it like any other bed: a material inspection, low-moisture dry vapor steam on the surface, seams and tufts, HEPA vacuuming and UV-C light treatment. Every visit includes a moisture check after service and the built-in bed mite sensor on our UV-C vacuum. Why those steps matter is covered in the [Sleep Sanitation Knowledge Center](https://sleepsanitation.com/knowledge-center).

## What changes in an Omaha winter?

Mostly the calendar. Omaha's outdoor air in January averages a 16°F dew point at Eppley Airfield, so a winter guest room isn't battling the summer humidity we write about in [summer humidity and basement bedrooms](/omaha-homes/humid-summers-and-basement-bedrooms). The rule stays the same in any season: the bed stays unmade until it is dry to the touch, and we confirm with a moisture check.

The other winter factor is trash day. If you are replacing the guest mattress instead, the city's contractor skips collection on Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day and runs a day late for the rest of that week. Mattresses don't go in the cart anyway; see [getting rid of a mattress in Omaha](/omaha-homes/getting-rid-of-a-mattress-in-omaha).
