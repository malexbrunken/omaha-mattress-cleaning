Spare rooms are common south of Omaha. In the Census Bureau's 2020–2024 estimates, 77% of Papillion homes and 66% of Bellevue homes have three or more bedrooms. A guest mattress that sat unused since last winter still collects dust, and the first person to sleep on it in months is usually family. A reset the week before they arrive means a fresh bed with no last-minute scramble.
